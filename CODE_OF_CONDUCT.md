# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Awesome WorkBuddy to be a useful, calm, and inclusive community. Treat people with respect, assume good faith while correcting mistakes, and focus criticism on ideas, code, sources, and behavior rather than identity. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, dox, impersonate, spam, or publish someone else's private information.

Content that promotes credential theft, unsafe account access, malicious code, or evasion of platform safeguards may be removed even when it is technically relevant.

## Reporting

For a conduct concern, open a [new issue](https://github.com/sandbaseai/awesome-workbuddy/issues/new/choose) with the \`conduct\` prefix and include only details safe to make public. Do not include private personal data, credentials, or exploit details. If the report is sensitive, use the maintainer contact options on the [repository owner profile](https://github.com/sandbaseai) and do not post the details publicly.

Maintainers will review reports, protect the privacy of reporters where possible, and may edit, hide, close, or remove content; restrict participation; or report serious threats to the relevant service. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and explained when doing so does not create additional harm.

This standard applies in repository Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests, linked project spaces, and other community spaces when someone is representing this project.

## Scope

Questions about resource quality belong in Issues; open-ended ideas and peer help belong in [Discussions](https://github.com/sandbaseai/awesome-workbuddy/discussions). For contribution rules, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
