# Review Checklist

Use only the sections relevant to the deliverable.

## Evidence and accuracy

- Claims are supported by supplied sources or explicitly labeled as analysis.
- Quotations match the source and attribution points to the correct author or document.
- Numbers can be reproduced from cited data, formulas, or intermediate calculations.
- Dates, currencies, units, percentages, names, and version numbers are unambiguous.
- Time-sensitive information includes a check date.

## Structure and completeness

- The result answers the actual request and includes every named deliverable.
- The information hierarchy matches the audience's decisions and expected reading order.
- Introductions, conclusions, tables, appendices, and references agree with the body.
- Assumptions, exclusions, limitations, and unresolved questions are visible.

## Links and references

- Links open the intended public or authorized destination.
- Anchor text identifies the destination without relying on “here” or a raw URL.
- Internal headings, page references, figure numbers, and table numbers resolve correctly.
- Citations distinguish primary sources from commentary and secondary reporting.

## Visual and format checks

- The final artifact renders without clipping, overlap, overflow, missing fonts, or broken media.
- Type size, contrast, spacing, alignment, and page density remain readable at normal viewing size.
- Tables and charts have titles, units, labels, legends, and identifiable data sources.
- Spreadsheet formulas cover the intended range and do not contain unexpected errors or hard-coded substitutes.
- Slides have one clear message each and do not depend on speaker knowledge that is absent from notes or content.

## Safety and usability

- The output contains no exposed credentials, private data, hidden tracked changes, or unintended metadata.
- Instructions do not require unsafe permissions or destructive actions without an explicit warning.
- Legal, medical, financial, compliance, and education-assessment outputs state the need for qualified review.
- A reader can identify the next action, owner, or decision when the deliverable is operational.
